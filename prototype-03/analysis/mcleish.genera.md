### Genus: Psilochilus Barb. Rodr.
*Source: page-050*

**SUMMARY (uncertain):**
- Terrestrial or epiphytic, plants with creeping rhizomes. This is a monotypic genus of tropical America.

**Roots:**
- Roots adventitious, fleshy.

**Leaves:**
- Leaves membranaceous with lax petioles.

**Inflorescences:**
- Inflorescences terminal, a short spicate raceme.

**Flowers:**
- Flowers few.

**Sepals:**
- Sepals free, keeled along the mid-vein.

**Petals:**
- Petals free, not keeled along mid-vein.

**Lip:**
- Lip free, 3-lobed; disc with 3 longitudinal calli.

**Column:**
- Column elongate, clavate; anther terminal, 2-celled, mobile; pollinia 4.

**ETYMOLOGY:**
- From the Greek psilo (smooth) and chilus (lipped) in reference to the appearance of the lip.

#### Species: Psilochilus macrophyllus
*(Lindl.) Ames, Orch.*

**SUMMARY:**
- Fasc. 7: 45 (1922). Type: locality unknown, Linden (holo. K-LINDL.!). Pogonia macrophylla Lindl. in Ann. Mag. Nat. Hist. ser.3, 1: 335 (1858). P. physurifolia Rchb. f. in Nederl. Kruidk. Arch. 4: 324 (1858). Plant terrestrial or epiphytic, with fleshy roots. (Fig. 18).

**Stem:**
- Stem branched, ascending.

**Leaves:**
- Leaves rigid, membranaceous, oval-elliptic, up to 8 cm long and 5 cm wide.

**Inflorescence:**
- Inflorescence terminal, racemose, 1- to 2-flowered; bracts leafy, up to 2 cm long and 1.5 cm wide.

**Flowers:**
- Flowers ringent with greenish sepals, greenish- white petals and white lip.

**Sepals:**
- Sepals stiff and fleshy; dorsal sepal up to 1.9 cm long and 3 mm wide; lateral sepals up to 1.8 cm long and 3 mm wide.

**Petals:**
- Petals thinner than the sepals, up to 1.8 cm long and 3 mm wide.

**Lip:**
- Lip 3-lobed, up to 1.6 cm long and 8 mm wide; lateral lobes acute; mid-lobe with serrate margins.

**Column:**
- Column with 2 conspicuous broad apical teeth on the side of the rostellum.

**GENERAL DISTRIBUTION:**
- The West Indies, Central America to Panama.

**DISTRIBUTION IN BELIZE:**
- Cayo, Stann Creek and Toledo Districts. Uncommon.

**HABITAT:**
- On leaf litter over sandy soil on the forest floor and on mossy tree branches about 3 m above the ground.

**FLOWERING SEASON:**
- December.

**ETYMOLOGY:**
- From the Greek macrophyllus (large-leaved).

**NOTE:**
- Reported by Catling & Catling (1988).

### Genus: Epistephium Kunth
*Source: page-050*

**SUMMARY (uncertain):**
- Terrestrial herbs. A genus of about 20 species known from Belize, Trinidad and northern South America. One is known from Belize.

**Stems:**
- Stems tall, simple or branched.

**Leaves:**
- Leaves chartaceous to coriaceous, usually sessile or cordate-clasping, strongly veined.

**Inflorescences:**
- Inflorescences terminal or, rarely, axillary, racemose, many-flowered.

**Flowers:**
- Flowers large; bracts short; apex of ovary with an urceolate cup.

**Sepals:**
- Sepals subequal, free.

**Petals:**
- Petals rather similar to sepals but sometimes larger.

**Lip:**
- Lip adnate to the column, obovate, simple or slightly 3-lobed, enclasping the base of the column.

**Column:**
- Column long, dilated above, broadly 3-lobed at apex.

**Capsule:**
- Capsule oblongcylindric.

**ETYMOLOGY:**
- From the Greek epi (upon, above) and stefos (a crown, wreath) in reference to the crown-like growth that remains at the apex of the ovary.

**SUMMARY:**
- silica 12(2): 42, t.31 (1945). E minutum Barb.-Rodr. ex Hoehne, Fl. Brasilica 12(2): 42(1945). Plant terrestrial, erect, up to 30 cm tall, with fleshy roots and simple stems of up to 3 mm in diameter. (Fig. 19).

**Leaves:**
- Leaves thinly coriaceous, glabrous, veins rather prominent on both sides, short-petiolate, elliptic or oblong-elliptic, abruptly acute or acuminate, basally narrowed, up to 6.5 cm long and 3 cm wide.

**Inflorescence:**
- Inflorescence terminal or axillary, racemose, shorter than the leaves, up to 12-flowered.

**Flowers:**
- Flowers green; ovary arcuate, slightly trigonous, up to 4 cm long; bracts triangular, acuminate, persisting in fruit.

**Sepals:**
- Sepals spathulate, acute, up to 2 cm long and 3 mm wide.

**Petals:**
- Petals spathulate, up to 2 cm long and 4 mm wide, white.

**Lip:**
- Lip with lower clasping and adnate to the column, upper ⅓ dilated, rotundate in outline, entire, expanded portion up to 8 mm long, ciliate, upper half flesly-pilose.

**Column:**
- Column up to 17 mm long and 1.5 mm in diameter.

**Capsule:**
- Capsule linear-cylindric, up to 3.5 cm long and 2 mm in diameter.

- The West Indies, Belize, Venezuela, Brazil and Trinidad.
